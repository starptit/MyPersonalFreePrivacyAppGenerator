Privacy Policy

App: Neko English (iOS & iPadOS · com.toeic.NekoEnglish)
Last Updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neko English handles information when you use the mobile application. Neko English is designed as an offline-first TOEIC Reading and Vocabulary study app and does not require you to create an account.

Summary: Your study progress, test history, flashcards, and preferences stay locally on your device. We do not operate user accounts or collect personal profiles. If you use the free ad-supported version, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) process limited technical and advertising data as described below.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Neko English stores the following app data solely on your device so your study progress persists between sessions:

  • Study & Progress Data: Reading practice answers, draft sessions, completed test history, vocabulary mastery status, and favorited words.
  • App Preferences: Selected appearance mode, flashcard round size, auto-pronounce setting, speech speed, and optional daily study reminder time.
  • Local Diagnostic Events: A rolling local log (capped on-device) of in-app study and ad-lifecycle events used only for local app behavior; this log is not uploaded to a developer server.

You can delete all locally stored study data at any time inside the app via Settings → Reset Learning Data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Unless you purchase the one-time Remove Ads option, Neko English displays banner and interstitial ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and manages regional privacy consent using Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

  • No App Tracking Transparency (IDFA) Prompt: Neko English does not request access to Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) via App Tracking Transparency and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Data Processed by Google AdMob: When ads are requested, Google's SDK may automatically collect or receive technical information such as your IP address (used to infer general/coarse location), device model, OS version, app version, ad interaction/impression events, crash/performance diagnostics, and Apple SKAdNetwork attribution signals.
  • Consent Management: In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK, or Switzerland), Google UMP presents a consent form before requesting ads. You can review or update your consent choices anytime in Settings → Ad Privacy Options (when available in your region).

For more details on how Google processes data in AdMob, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use our services.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

Neko English offers an optional one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase processed directly by Apple App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect, receive, or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Apple provides only the purchase verification status needed to unlock ad-free access and restore your purchase on your devices.

4. Study Reminders (Local Notifications)

If you choose to enable daily study reminders in Settings, Neko English requests iOS notification permission and schedules local notifications directly on your device using Apple's UNUserNotificationCenter. No push notification server or remote device push token is used.

5. Speech & Offline Content

Vocabulary pronunciation uses Apple's on-device text-to-speech synthesizer (AVSpeechSynthesizer). Reading passages, questions, and the vocabulary dictionary are bundled offline inside the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Neko English is a general-audience English study application and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Other Regions)

Because Neko English does not collect or store your personal data on developer servers, you can exercise control directly on your device:

  • Delete Local Data: Use Settings → Reset Learning Data or uninstall the app.
  • Manage Ad Consent: Use Settings → Ad Privacy Options (where applicable) or purchase Remove Ads to stop all ad requests.
  • Manage Notifications: Disable reminders in app Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
